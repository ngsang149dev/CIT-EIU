EIGROSS:
NET: after-tax income
GROSS: before-tax income
TAXRATE: tax rate
	Formulas:
TAX=NET/((100 - TAXRATE)/10)
NET=(1-TAXRATE)*GROSS 
GROSS = NET/(1-TAXRATE)

S=P+I (principal + interest)/(gốc + lãi)
rate: tỉ lệ lãi suất, n: tháng
Lãi đơn(Simple Interest):
General formula: P*(1 +rate *n)

Lãi kép(Compound Interest)
General formula: P*(1 +rate)^n
=> n = log(1+rate)(S/P)
=> months = (log(S/P)) / (log1+rate)
E.g: result = (Math.log(expectedMoney / money)) / (Math.log(1 + (interestRate / 100)));
